# Code of conduct

ScreenGarden welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and experience.
Be kind, specific and patient. Discuss the work and its evidence. Give feedback
that helps someone improve; respect privacy and personal boundaries.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, sexual attention, doxxing and sharing
private information without consent are unacceptable. Do not shame people for their
screen habits or demand personal screen-time data to prove an issue.

These expectations apply to issues, pull requests, discussions and other project
spaces. Maintainers may edit/remove harmful content, close a conversation, or
restrict participation. Responses should be proportionate and explain the
applicable rule while protecting affected people's privacy.

For a conduct concern, contact [34rns](https://github.com/34rns) through an available
private GitHub contact route. If none is available, open a minimal issue asking for
private contact; do not post incident details or personal information publicly.
Reports will be handled with care and discretion.
