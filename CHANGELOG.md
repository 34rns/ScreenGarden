# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- One shared deterministic renderer for World, Focus, Desk, widgets, wallpaper and snapshots.
- Pan and zoom across a growing field, with camera buttons and a complete plant list.
- A seed after each good completed day; older plantings stay in the world.
- Fullscreen Focus in your own field, with a small timer and exit without cancellation.
- Opt-in dim Desk mode, optional clock, slow pixel shifting and a charging offer.
- Refreshed green/dusk palettes, system typography, shapes and original meadow scenery.
- Backup format v3 with tested v1/v2 defaults for the new Desk preferences.
- RTL tap-target alignment, window restoration and large-world wallpaper dock clearance.
- Reliable live reduced-motion updates and plant roots aligned with the meadow horizon.
- Fullscreen cutout coverage, readable Desk clock contrast and correct English clock order in RTL.
- Room remains v7; published schemas, permissions and focus reward rules are unchanged.

Real-device power use, heat, OLED wear and manufacturer behavior remain unverified.
Pixel shifting reduces static exposure but cannot guarantee protection from burn-in.

## 0.1.0

Initial signed GitHub release: offline garden, focus, habits, insights, plant packs,
local backups, widgets, Quick Settings tile, wallpaper and optional blocking.
